# Senior Product Manager, Carrier Platform: Kestrel Logistics

Saved 2026-09-22 from kestrel-logistics.example/careers (fictional)

Kestrel builds the platform carriers use to plan, run and get paid for their loads. Carriers
shouldn't need to call anyone to know where their load stands, and neither should the shippers
who hired them.

**What you'll do**
- Own the carrier-facing platform: load status, notifications, proof of delivery
- Talk to dispatchers and drivers every week
- Set outcome goals with two cross-functional teams

**What you bring**
- 8+ years in product management, ideally in logistics or B2B platforms
- Hands-on discovery: you run user interviews yourself
- Comfortable with SQL and product analytics
- Fluent English; German is a plus

Munich, hybrid (two office days a week).
