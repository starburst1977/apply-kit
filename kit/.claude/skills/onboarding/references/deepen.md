# Deepen: steps 5 to 7, and profile updates

Optional. Run only when the user asks ("deepen my profile"). Each step stands alone: the user
can do one now and the rest another day. Say roughly how long each takes before starting.

## Step 5: Role interviews (about 3 minutes per role)

Most recent role first. Roles older than about 15 years get one combined question at the end.
For each role, ask only what `experience.md` doesn't already answer, in one message:

"Let's talk about {title} at {company}. A few sentences each is plenty:
1. What was the product or service, and who used it?
2. What did you personally do, as opposed to the team? Your CV says '{a bullet}'. What did
   that look like day to day?
3. What changed because of you? A decision, a launch, a fix, a habit that stuck.
4. Anything from that time you're proud of that isn't on your CV?
5. The hardest problem there, and how you handled it?"

Write the answers under the role, in the user's words, tightened but not embellished:
`**Product and users:**`, `**What I did:**`, `**What changed:**`, `**Stories:**`.
New numbers go under `**Numbers:**` tagged `[unconfirmed]`.

After each role: "Next role, or a break?" Under Notes, keep a list of roles done, so another
session can continue with the next one. Tick step 5 when every role in the last 15 years is done.

## Step 6: Numbers audit (about 5 minutes)

1. List every number in `experience.md` with its current tag, grouped by role.
2. For each one, ask where it comes from, and offer the answers:
   - Measured: how and when? ("pilot with 40 users, spring 2023")
   - From a report or survey: which one?
   - Estimate: then it may appear only phrased as one ("roughly", "around"), or not at all.
   - Don't know any more: it stays `[unconfirmed]` and is never used.
3. Write the result as `[source: …]`, in the user's words.
4. If the same thing appears with two different values (4 product lines here, 8 there), ask
   which is right and fix every place it appears.
5. Tick step 6.

## Step 7: Voice sample (about 3 minutes)

1. Ask: "Paste something you wrote yourself, a few paragraphs: an email, a post, a cover letter
   you liked. Something you wrote without AI, so I learn how you sound."
2. Then, in one message: "Three quick ones: 1. Formal or relaxed? 2. For German letters to
   younger companies, Sie or du? 3. Anything you'd never say about yourself?"
3. Write `profile/voice.md`: the sample, the answers, and under "Observed" three to five plain
   notes on how they write: sentence length, how direct they are, how they open and close,
   words they use a lot. Describe; don't judge.
4. Tick step 7.

## Updating the profile ("update my profile")

- New job, promotion, new qualification: add or change the entry in `experience.md`, same
  rules as on import. New numbers start `[unconfirmed]`.
- Show the change back in one or two lines before saving.
- If `CV_General.md` is affected, offer to update it and render it again.
