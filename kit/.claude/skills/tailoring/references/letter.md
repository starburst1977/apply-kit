# Writing the letter

One page, 250 to 350 words, in the user's voice (`voice.md`). It doesn't repeat the CV: it says
why *this* role, and proves it with two or three pieces of evidence.

## Language and form

- **German:** `Anschreiben_<Company>.md`, Sie-form, unless the company clearly uses du (many
  startups do; the ad shows it) or `voice.md` says otherwise.
- **English:** `CoverLetter_<Company>.md`.
- German when the ad is in German or the company is a German-speaking business; English for
  English ads and international teams, even at German companies.
- **Formal fields** (frontmatter, see `guide/format.md`): German letters always get `date` and
  `subject`, plus `to` when the ad gives an address or contact person. English letters: `date`
  and `subject` for established companies, optional for startups. Use `date: today`.

## Salutation

- Named contact in the ad: "Sehr geehrte Frau Weber," / "Dear Ms Weber,". For relaxed
  companies, or if `voice.md` says so: "Hallo Frau Weber,".
- No name: address the team. "Liebes Acme-Team," / "Dear Acme team,". Avoid "Sehr geehrte
  Damen und Herren" and "To whom it may concern".
- German: after the salutation's comma, the letter continues in lower case.

## Structure

1. **Opening, two or three sentences.** Something specific and true about the company, its
   product or the role, from the ad or the company's site, and why it connects to the user's
   own experience. Not "I'm excited to apply". Not a summary of the CV.
2. **Evidence, one or two paragraphs.** The two or three most relevant things from
   `experience.md`, told briefly, with what changed. Use stories from the deepening step if
   there are any. The ad's words, where true.
3. **How they work, or why this team.** One short, concrete paragraph.
4. **Practical, one or two sentences, only if relevant:** location or remote, languages, and
   what the ad asks for: salary expectation (from `targeting.md`), earliest start date (ask
   the user if it's not known).
5. **Close, one sentence.** Then the sign-off: the closing, and the name on the next line.
   - English: "Kind regards," or "Best regards,".
   - German: "Mit freundlichen Grüßen" (formal), "Viele Grüße" or "Beste Grüße" (modern).
     No comma.

## Years of experience

**Years of experience** may be worked out from the confirmed dates in `experience.md`: round
down ("over 25 years", "über 25 Jahre") and never count parallel roles twice. The first time,
record it under Contact as `- **Years of experience:** over 25 [source: derived from your
confirmed timeline, Dec 1999 to today]`, so the numbers check can see it.

## Tone

- Direct and confident. Never arrogant, never apologetic.
- Show, don't claim: no "excellent communicator", "passionate", "team player", "hard-working".
- Gaps: don't apologise. Show adjacent real experience if there is some, or leave it out.
- No em dashes. Short paragraphs, each a single line in the file.
- One idea per paragraph. A personal note (an interest, a value) gets its own sentence or two,
  not a place at the start of an unrelated paragraph.
- Using the user's own answer in another language: keep what they meant, and phrase it the way
  a native speaker would. Never translate word for word.
