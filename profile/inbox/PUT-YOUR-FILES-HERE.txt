Put your current CV in this folder as a PDF.

Also useful, if you have them:
- Your LinkedIn profile as a PDF (on your profile page: More, then Save to PDF).
  Important: before you save it, click every "see more" under your roles and your About
  text, and "Show all experiences" if some roles are hidden. LinkedIn only prints what is
  open on screen, so anything still collapsed arrives cut off mid-sentence.
- Older CVs
- Cover letters you have written

A Word file? Open it and choose File, then Save as PDF (or Export as PDF).
