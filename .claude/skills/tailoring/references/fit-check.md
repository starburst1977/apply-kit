# Fit check

Fast and honest. The user decides whether to apply; the job here is to make that easy.

## Read the ad for

- Company, role title, level, team or product
- Location, remote or hybrid, the language of the ad and of the team
- Must-haves ("required", "you have") and nice-to-haves ("bonus", "ideally")
- Their exact words for the core skills
- What they ask for: CV, cover letter, salary expectation, start date, portfolio, contact
  person, deadline
- Salary range, if stated

## Compare with the profile

- Each must-have: where is it evidenced in `experience.md` (which role, which fact)? Or missing?
- `targeting.md`: role, level, industries, location, languages, dealbreakers. Any clash?

## Show the verdict

```
**Acme Payments: Senior Product Manager.**
Skills: strong fit. Role: matches your target.

Matches
- "B2B payments experience" → Product Manager, Beta Bank (merchant onboarding)
- "Data-informed decisions, SQL" → Acme: weekly funnel analysis

Gaps
- "Experience with card issuing": not in your profile

Watch out
- Hybrid, 3 days a week in Berlin; your targeting says Munich or remote

They want: CV, cover letter, salary expectation, earliest start date
```

Then one line of honest advice, and: "Shall I write the CV and letter?"

Two separate judgements, because "can you do it" and "is it what you want" differ:

- **Skills:** strong / good / partial / weak. How many must-haves are evidenced in
  `experience.md`. Partial: say what the letter has to carry.
- **Role:** matches your target / below it / above it / off target. Level, salary, location,
  industry against `targeting.md`, plus any dealbreaker.

A strong skills fit for a role below target is a real finding, not a reason to be
encouraging. If either judgement is weak, say so plainly and advise against, unless the user
has a reason. It's their call.

Keep it short. It's a decision aid, not a report.

## Before sending (for the hand-over)

- Company and contact names spelled right
- Everything they asked for is included: salary expectation, start date, portfolio link
- Both PDFs opened and looked at, not just generated
- Sent the way the ad asks: portal, email address, reference number
