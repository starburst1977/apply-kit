Examples: Lena Hoffmann, a fictional person

Everything in this folder is invented, to show what the kit produces:
- profile/experience.md   what a finished profile looks like, with sourced numbers
- applications/ads/       the two job ads she applied to
- applications/           her tailored CV, an English cover letter, a German Anschreiben
- output/                 the PDFs the kit made from them

Your own profile and documents live in the profile/ and applications/ folders one level up.
